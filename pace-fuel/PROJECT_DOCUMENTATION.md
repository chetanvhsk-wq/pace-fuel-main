# Viorra: The Elite AI Wellness Ecosystem
## Project Documentation & Planning Blueprint

---

## 1. Project Vision
**Viorra** is a high-performance wellness application designed for athletes and fitness enthusiasts who demand precision, data-driven insights, and personalized coaching. It moves beyond simple tracking by integrating advanced AI (Gemini) to analyze nutrition, generate optimal training strategies, and provide real-time wellness coaching.

### Core Objectives:
- **Precision Tracking**: Accurate logging of workouts, nutrition, hydration, and mindfulness.
- **AI-First Intelligence**: Leveraging Gemini for image-based food analysis and personalized plan generation.
- **Holistic Wellness**: Balancing physical training with mental recovery and sleep optimization.
- **Native Experience**: A seamless cross-platform experience (Web & Mobile via Capacitor).

---

## 2. Product Ecosystem (Core Pillars)

### A. The Performance Dashboard
The central hub for daily metrics.
- **Dynamic Health Score**: A real-time calculation (0-100) based on calorie balance, hydration, step count, and mindfulness progress.
- **Quick Stats**: At-a-glance view of daily goals vs. actuals.
- **AI Insights**: Contextual encouragement and advice generated by the AI Sports Scientist.

### B. Training & Performance
- **Exercise Logging**: Manual and template-based logging with support for muscle group targeting.
- **AI Plan Generator**: Custom workout plans generated based on the user's "Athlete Type", available equipment, and specific goals.
- **Muscle Group Analysis**: Tracking volume and intensity across different muscle groups.

### C. Nutrition & Wellness
- **AI Food Analysis**: Users can upload photos of their meals; Gemini identifies the food (specialized in Indian cuisine) and estimates macros.
- **Hydration Tracker**: Visual progress towards daily water goals.
- **Mindfulness & Recovery**: Meditation timers, mood tracking, and AI-generated meditation scripts.
- **Supplement Log**: Tracking daily intake of vitamins and performance supplements.

### D. Progress & Analytics
- **Weekly Trends**: Visualizing calorie intake, weight changes, and workout volume using Recharts.
- **Personal Bests**: Tracking and celebrating strength and endurance milestones.
- **AI Strategy**: Periodic analysis of performance data to recommend macro-nutrient adjustments or training intensity shifts.

## 4. Data Architecture (Firestore Schema)

### `UserProfile`
- `uid`: Unique identifier.
- `athleteType`: endurance, strength, hybrid, general.
- `goal`: performance, weight-loss, muscle-gain, maintenance.
- `dietPreference`: north-indian, south-indian, vegetarian, etc.
- `dailyGoals`: Calories, Water, Protein, Carbs, Fats, Steps.

### `FoodLogEntry`
- `userId`, `name`, `calories`, `protein`, `carbs`, `fats`, `mealType`, `timestamp`, `imageUrl`.

### `ExerciseEntry`
- `userId`, `name`, `duration`, `caloriesBurned`, `intensity`, `muscleGroup`, `timestamp`.

### `WellnessSession`
- `userId`, `type` (meditation/sleep), `duration`, `score`, `timestamp`.

## 5. AI Integration Strategy

Viorra utilizes the `@google/genai` SDK for several critical features:

1.  **Vision Analysis**: `gemini-2.5-flash-preview` for rapid food identification and macro estimation from images.
2.  **Personalized Planning**: `gemini-3-flash-preview` for generating complex exercise and nutrition strategies based on user BMI, age, and goals.
3.  **Conversational Coaching**: "Viorra" (the AI Coach) uses a persistent chat session to provide science-based advice.
4.  **Content Generation**: Real-time generation of meditation scripts tailored to the user's current mood.

## 6. UI/UX Design Philosophy

The app follows a **"Hardware Specialist Tool"** aesthetic:
- **Mood**: Professional, focused, and high-tech.
- **Typography**: Inter (Sans) for UI, JetBrains Mono for data/metrics.
- **Color Palette**: Dark matte surfaces with vibrant accent glows (Success Green, Warning Orange, Performance Blue).
- **Interactions**: Tactile feedback via Framer Motion, glassmorphism overlays, and visible grid structures.

## 8. Build & Deployment Instructions

### Web Deployment
1.  Ensure `.env` contains `GEMINI_API_KEY` and Firebase config.
2.  Run `npm run build`.
3.  Deploy the `dist/` folder to your hosting provider.

### Android APK Generation (Local Machine)
1.  Install Android Studio and Java JDK 17.
2.  Run `npm run build`.
3.  Run `npx cap sync android`.
4.  Open the `android` project in Android Studio.
5.  Build APK via **Build > Build Bundle(s) / APK(s) > Build APK(s)**.
